TreeLan — Skill‑First AI Freelance Economy

TreeLan is a skill‑first freelancing platform designed to help beginners and underrepresented learners earn through verified abilities rather than past experience. The platform introduces AI‑assisted testing sessions that evaluate how effectively users generate high‑quality output — even when AI tools are used.


Instead of restricting AI usage, TreeLan embraces it and designs assessments that measure creativity, reasoning, and execution. The goal is to build a learning‑to‑earning ecosystem where users learn skills, prove them through structured tests, and immediately access paid opportunities.


TreeLan aims to create a new economy where AI and humans work together productively, reducing hiring risk for clients while opening doors for beginner freelancers across domains.

SoulSpace — Student Mental Health Platform

SoulSpace is a mental health monitoring platform designed for higher‑grade students. The system analyzes behavioral patterns using validated psychological assessments such as PHQ‑9 and GAD‑7.


It includes a chatbot named Serene that monitors daily inputs, detects potential risk signals, and generates alerts when concerning behavioral patterns appear. The project ranked in the top 9 among 200 teams.

SewerOne — Autonomous Sludge Cleaning Robot

SewerOne is a sludge cleaning robot designed for sewage treatment plants to reduce human exposure to hazardous environments. The robot uses ROS2 Humble with Nav2 navigation and SLAM mapping for autonomous movement.


I implemented Gazebo simulation and RViz2 SLAM using LiDAR sensors and odometry. The system autonomously maps confined spaces and navigates around obstacles. A functional prototype was built for an industry‑hosted hackathon.


The project won 1st place and secured an internship opportunity at Genex Utility, demonstrating its practical value and deployment potential.
